Unusual Foods Around Us

Discover unique from South Africa and around the world.

Why we Chose This Theme

Our group chose the theme Unsusual Foods because food is an important aspect of our everyday lives. Many people are unaware of the unique foods that exist Around different communities around the world. We wanted to educate people about these foods, their cultural importance and encourage people to explore different cuisines.

How we gathered the information

We gathered information through online research from reliable food and cultural food websites, discussions with people familiar with these foods and photographs.We compared information from different sources to ensure accuracy
